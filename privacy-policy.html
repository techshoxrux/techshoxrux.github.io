Privacy Policy — Kitsune Jewels (きつねの宝石)

Effective Date: November 15, 2026 • Package ID: com.kitsunejewels.app

Welcome to Kitsune Jewels ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you play the Kitsune Jewels mobile game on Android devices.

1. Information We Collect

Local Game Progress: Your game progress (puzzle levels, stars, coins, and panda sanctuary farm state) is stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. We do not require you to create an account or submit personal details such as your name, address, or phone number.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

Kitsune Jewels uses Google AdMob (`com.google.android.gms.ads`) to display banner and optional rewarded video advertisements. Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain non-personal and device-level data, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID)
  • Coarse / approximate location (derived from IP address)
  • Ad interaction and diagnostic performance data (ad views, clicks, crash/performance logs)

You can learn more about how Google uses data from apps that use its services by visiting Google's Privacy & Terms. Users may reset or opt out of personalized advertising at any time in their Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

3. Children's Privacy

Kitsune Jewels is a family-friendly match-3 puzzle and panda sanctuary simulation game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Any advertising served complies with Google Play Families and applicable data protection policies.

4. Data Security & Retention

Because your gameplay save data is stored locally on your device, you can clear or delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the application. Data processed by Google AdMob is governed by Google's data retention and security policies.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our official GitHub repository page.
